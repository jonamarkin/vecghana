Our Story & Essence

A decade-long legacy of inspiring voices, uplifting spirits, and proclaiming the Gospel through Ghanaian and classical choral mastery.

The Foundation

Founded on Faith & Musical Passion

Founded in 2013 in Dansoman, Accra, VocalEssence Chorale Ghana (VEC Ghana) was birthed with a resolute mission: to provide a vibrant platform for talented young Christian musicians to minister the Gospel with musical precision and unyielding passion.

Over the last 13+ years, the choir has blossomed into one of Ghana’s most respected interdenominational choral institutions. From intimate church sanctuaries to national stages and international choral festivals, VocalEssence Chorale blends rich Ghanaian traditional medleys, highlife choral rhythms, Western classical anthems, and spirit-filled contemporary worship.

Beyond the music, we are a family dedicated to Christian fellowship, character building, vocal education, and tangible humanitarian outreach that lifts communities.

Milestones

A Journey of Grace & Impact

Key moments and milestone achievements across our 13+ years of choral ministry.

2013

Founding in Dansoman, Accra

VocalEssence Chorale was birthed by a passionate group of young choristers committed to spreading the Gospel through choral music.

2017

Debut Album: Nyame Ye Osahen

Released our first landmark album featuring spirit-filled anthems like Nyame Ye Osahen and Awurade W'ahenie So.

2020

Oman Ghana & National Recognition

Released the iconic patriotic compilation Oman Ghana featuring all 3 stanzas of the Ghana National Anthem and Ghana Nyigba.

October 2023

7th Africa Sings Festival in Cotonou, Benin

Represented Ghana on the continental stage at the pan-African choral festival, fostering cross-cultural unity through African harmonies.

2023 - 2024

Harmony of a Decade & Saving Hearts Project

Celebrated our 10th anniversary with the live concert album Harmony of a Decade and partnered with Korle Bu Cardiothoracic Unit for the Saving Hearts Project.

2025 - 2026

Ayeyi Ndwom & Worldwide Streaming Expansion

Released Ayeyi Ndwom and expanded streaming reach across Apple Music, YouTube, TikTok, and global digital platforms.
