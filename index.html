International Milestone

Representing Ghana at the 7th Africa Sings Festival

In October 2023, VocalEssence Chorale Ghana took center stage in Cotonou, Benin, participating in the prestigious 7th Edition of the Africa Sings Festival. The pan-African festival united top choral groups across the continent to celebrate African harmonies, foster cultural unity, and glorify God.
