10th Anniversary Landmark Project

The Saving Hearts Project with Korle Bu

As part of our 10th-anniversary celebration (*Harmony of a Decade*), VocalEssence Chorale Ghana launched the Saving Hearts Project in collaboration with the National Cardiothoracic Centre & Children's Unit at the Korle Bu Teaching Hospital.

The initiative raised crucial awareness and financial contributions to sponsor life-saving heart surgeries for children diagnosed with congenital heart disease, bringing hope and smiles back to families in need.
