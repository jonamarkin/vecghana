Frequently Asked Questions

Booking & Engagement FAQs

How early should we book the choir?

We recommend booking at least 4 to 8 weeks in advance, especially for wedding seasons (November–January, Easter) and major holiday periods. However, we do accommodate expedited requests subject to availability.

Can we request specific songs or medleys?

Yes! You can choose from our extensive repertoire of classical anthems, Ghanaian highlife medleys, and sacred hymns, or request custom choral arrangements for your special event.

Do you travel outside Accra and Ghana?

Absolutely. VocalEssence Chorale ministers across all 16 regions of Ghana and internationally, as demonstrated during our tour at the Africa Sings Festival in Benin. Logistics arrangements are discussed during booking.

How do auditions and joining the choir work?

We hold periodic auditions for talented Christian singers and instrumentalists. Select "General Inquiry / Audition" in the booking form above and include your vocal part to receive the next audition date.
