Continental Representation

7th Edition of Africa Sings Festival | Cotonou, Benin

VocalEssence Chorale Ghana was honored to represent the nation of Ghana at the 7th Edition of the Africa Sings Festival in Cotonou, Republic of Benin (October 11–15, 2023).

This high-profile pan-African festival convened premier choral ensembles from across West Africa and beyond. Our choir performed dynamic Ghanaian choral works, African highlife arrangements, and sacred anthems, fostering cultural unity and glorifying God through African harmonies.

Feliz Navidad Christmas Concert Series

Feliz Navidad is VocalEssence Chorale's premier annual Christmas experience (spanning acclaimed editions including Feliz Navidad 9, Feliz Navidad XI, and Feliz Navidad 12th Noel). It brings families and music enthusiasts together for an evening of festive grandeur, classical carols, Ghanaian highlife arrangements, and nativity drama.
